Swiss Travel Guide Site Plan

Site Name

Swiss Travel Guide

The name was chosen because it clearly represents a website dedicated to providing travel information about Switzerland, including destinations, attractions, and planning resources.

Site Purpose

This website provides information about popular travel destinations in Switzerland such as Zurich, Geneva, and the Swiss Alps. It offers travel tips, estimated costs, and a contact form to help users plan their trips.

Scenarios

  • What are the best places to visit in Switzerland and how much do they cost?
  • How can I contact the site for travel recommendations about Switzerland?

Color Schema

Teal: Used for header, footer, and headings.

Light Teal (#b2f5ea): Used for accents and borders.

Very Light Teal (#f0fdfa): Used as background color.

Typography

Montserrat: Used for headings.

Roboto: Used for body text.
