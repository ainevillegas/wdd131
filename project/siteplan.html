Swiss Travel Guide - Site Plan

Site Name

Swiss Travel Guide

The name "Swiss Travel Guide" was chosen because the website focuses specifically on travel experiences, destinations, and planning tips within Switzerland. It clearly communicates the purpose of helping users explore Swiss cities, mountains, and attractions.

Site Purpose

The website provides information about popular travel destinations in Switzerland including cities like Zurich and Geneva, as well as scenic locations such as the Swiss Alps. It offers travel tips, estimated costs, and a contact form for inquiries, helping visitors plan their trips efficiently.

Scenarios

  • What are the best places to visit in Switzerland and how much do they cost?
  • How can I contact the site to ask about travel recommendations in Switzerland?

Color Schema

Teal: Used for headers, navigation bars, and headings.

Light Teal (#b2f5ea): Used for accents and highlights.

Very Light Teal Background (#f0fdfa): Used as the page background.

Typography

Montserrat: Used for headings and titles.

Roboto: Used for body content and paragraphs.
