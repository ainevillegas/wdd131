Travel Explorer Site Plan

Site Name

Travel Explorer

The name "Travel Explorer" was chosen because it represents a platform where users can discover and explore different travel destinations around the world.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide travel information, destination highlights, and travel planning resources. Users can explore different locations, view pricing information, and contact the site for inquiries.

Scenarios

  • What travel destinations are available and what are their prices?
  • How can I contact the site for travel inquiries or recommendations?

Color Schema

#1e3a8a (Dark Blue): Used for header, footer, and headings.

#fbbf24 (Gold): Used for accents such as borders and buttons.

#f9fafb (Light Gray): Used as the main background color.

Typography

Montserrat: Used for headings and titles.

Roboto: Used for body text and paragraphs.
